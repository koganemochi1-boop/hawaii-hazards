Privacy & data sources

When you enter an address, it goes to Nominatim, the open-source geocoder run by the OpenStreetMap Foundation. They use your address once to look up coordinates and may log your IP briefly. We don't send your address anywhere else and don't store it locally.

Hazard data is queried from the State of Hawaiʻi Statewide GIS Program and the FEMA Flood Map Service Center. Translation and action content are drafted from HI-EMA, USGS HVO, Hawaiʻi WMO, and Red Cross Hawaiʻi.
