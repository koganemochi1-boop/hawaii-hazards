About this map

Hazard layers & sources

  • FEMA Flood Zones (DFIRM) — State of Hawaiʻi Statewide GIS, Hazards/MapServer/6. Live ArcGIS REST query (zoom ≥ 10).
  • Wildfire Risk Areas — State of Hawaiʻi Statewide GIS, Hazards/MapServer/7. Live ArcGIS REST query.
  • Sea Level Rise + 1% Coastal Flood (3.2 ft) — Hazards/MapServer/15. Live ArcGIS REST.
  • Tsunami Evacuation Zones — bundled GeoJSON from Hazards/MapServer/11.
  • Lava Flow Hazard Zones (USGS 1–9) — bundled GeoJSON from Hazards/MapServer/3.

A note on hurricane storm surge

Hawaiʻi does not publish SLOSH-style hurricane storm-surge vector data. The combined "1% coastal flood + 3.2 ft sea level rise" layer is used here as the de facto coastal storm hazard scenario. Tsunami evacuation zones serve as the practical coastal evacuation framework during hurricanes per Hawaiʻi emergency management.

Composite risk score

Each layer contributes a weight × hit-score (0 to 1) to a composite. Weights: Lava 0.25, Tsunami 0.20, DFIRM 0.20, SLR + Coastal Flood 0.20, Wildfire 0.15. Buckets: 0–0.15 Low, 0.15–0.35 Moderate, 0.35–0.60 High, 0.60–0.85 Severe, 0.85+ Extreme.
